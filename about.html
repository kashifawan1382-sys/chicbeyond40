About Chic Beyond 40

PHOTO: Warm, friendly portrait-style image representing the Chic Beyond 40 reader — a smiling woman over 50 in a camel coat and cream knit, natural light

Chic Beyond 40 was created for a woman the fashion world keeps overlooking: over 40, over 50, over 60 — with a real body, a real life, and absolutely no interest in looking frumpy, invisible, or twenty years younger than she is.

If you have ever stood in front of your wardrobe before an ordinary Tuesday and thought, "Everything either squeezes my tummy or makes me look like I've given up," you are exactly who we write for.

What We Believe

  • Flattering beats hiding. Baggy, shapeless clothes do not camouflage a tummy — they add bulk everywhere. Skimming fabrics, vertical lines and a defined waist do the real work.
  • Comfort is not the opposite of style. Flat-front trousers with stretch, shoes you can walk in, knits that do not itch — polish and comfort belong in the same outfit.
  • Fewer, better pieces. Our capsule wardrobe guides help you build a wardrobe where everything works together, so getting dressed takes five calm minutes.
  • Age is a styling brief, not a limit. We write "over 40 / 50 / 60" versions of trends because proportions and priorities change — taste does not expire.

What You'll Find Here

Numbered, copy-them-today outfit guides for every season; capsule wardrobes that turn 12 pieces into weeks of outfits; work outfits that are polished and comfortable; and honest style guides — like how to wear a cardigan without looking frumpy — that explain why something works, so you can apply the rule to clothes you already own.

How We Make Money

Chic Beyond 40 is free to read. We earn through advertising and through affiliate links: when you buy something through a "Shop the Look" link, we may receive a small commission at no extra cost to you. That is fully explained in our Affiliate Disclosure, and it never changes what we recommend — every outfit here is one we would genuinely suggest to a friend.

Say Hello

Questions, outfit dilemmas, or a request for a guide you'd love to see? Visit our Contact page — we read everything. You can also join the free email list on the homepage for a new outfit idea every week and our printable Tummy-Friendly Capsule Checklist.
