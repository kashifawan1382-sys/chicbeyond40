Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Chic Beyond 40 ("we", "us", "our", the "Site", chicbeyond40.com) collects when you visit, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this Site you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us. If you subscribe to our email list, we collect your email address. If you contact us, we collect your name, email address and the contents of your message.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics providers may automatically record standard log information: your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent, and the page that referred you. This information is aggregated and used to understand which content readers enjoy.

2. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We and our partners use cookies to: remember your preferences, measure traffic and content performance, and serve advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the Site will still work, though some features may be limited.

3. Advertising — Google AdSense and Third Parties

We display advertising provided by third parties, including Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices (US) or youronlinechoices.com (UK/EU).

Other advertising or analytics partners we may use in future (for example Mediavine/Journey) operate under their own privacy policies and cookie practices, which will be reflected in updates to this page.

4. Affiliate Links

Some links on this Site are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, the retailer may use a cookie to attribute the sale to us so we can earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

5. Email List

If you subscribe, we use your email address only to send the content you signed up for (outfit ideas, checklists and occasional updates). Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from the list. We never sell your email address.

6. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as Google Analytics or our host's built-in statistics) to count visits and see which posts are most helpful. Analytics data is used in aggregate and is not used to identify individual visitors.

7. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the Site and its content;
  • To send the emails you subscribed to;
  • To respond to your messages;
  • To measure readership and serve advertising that keeps the Site free;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

8. Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information only with service providers who help us run the Site (hosting, email delivery, analytics and advertising), and only as necessary for them to provide their services, or when required by law.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including under the UK GDPR / EU GDPR and the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to opt out of personalised advertising as described above. To exercise any of these rights, contact us via the Contact page and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children's Privacy

This Site is written for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the UK/EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. External Links

This Site links to retailer and other third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites; please review their own policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy? Please use our Contact page.
